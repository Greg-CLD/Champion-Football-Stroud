Working rules

The 1v1 match
at a glance.

Fast to explain. Fair to play. Still subject to rehearsal, host-club agreement and Gloucestershire football-authority guidance.

01

Start every attack

The same one-two start and ten-second limit are working rules for every turn.

  1. Attacker passesPlay the ball to the defender.
  2. Defender returnsPlay a controlled, neutral pass back.
  3. Clock startsThe attacker’s first touch begins the ten seconds.
  4. Turn finishesGoal, defensive stop, save, foul stoppage, ball out or time.
If the return cannot be controlled

The official stops play before the clock starts and allows one retake. After a second unusable return, the official supplies a neutral rolling pass. An ordinary mistake does not change the score.

The one-two start and ten-second limit remain working rules to confirm during rehearsal.

02

Six-attack match

Each player gets three attacks. They alternate until all six attacks are complete.

Player A attacks first, then Player B. The players keep alternating until each has attacked three times and defended three times.

One designated goalkeeper stays in goal for the whole match. The same goalkeeper faces both players for all six attacks.

Working detail to confirm

The goalkeeper stays for this whole match. Whether the same goalkeeper continues into later matches remains to be decided.

03

Goal-only scoring

Only a goal adds one point to a player’s total.

Goal
1 point for the attacker.
Defence
No point is awarded. The stop prevents the attacker scoring.
Save
No point is awarded. The save prevents the attacker scoring.

There are no bonus points for skills, blocks, tackles or saves. A stopped attack simply finishes with no goal added.

04

Winning the match

The player with the most goals after six attacks advances to the next round.

  • The normal match ends after all six attacks.
  • The player with the most goals advances.
  • If the scores are level, the match moves to sudden death.
  • The exact sudden-death sequence needs confirmation through rehearsal.
  • The wider tournament pathway will be chosen after the match format has been tested.
Working details to confirm

Sudden death could use one paired attack each or a first-goal-wins sequence. The wider competition could be direct progression or include a second-chance route.

05

Child-friendly conduct

Keep the contest brave, safe and positive.

Ordinary mistakes

Stop, reset and play again without harsh automatic deductions.

Repeated deliberate fouls

Give a clear warning and let the pitch official manage the behaviour.

Dangerous conduct

The event lead may remove a child from play and return them to their parent or carer.

Touchline

Encouragement is welcome. Live technical instructions are not.
