Match rules

The 1v1 match
at a glance.

Fast to explain. Fair to play. These rules apply to every Stroud 1v1 match.

3/5each
Start Match Scoring Result Conduct
01

Start every attack

Each attack follows the same one-two start. There is no time limit.

  1. Attacker passesPlay the ball to the defender.
  2. Defender returnsPlay a controlled, neutral pass back.
  3. Attack beginsThe live duel begins on the attacker’s first touch.
  4. Go endsA goal, a goalkeeper collection, the defender kicking the ball out or the ball otherwise going out ends the go. A saved rebound stays live.
If the return cannot be controlled

The official stops play before the attack begins and allows one retake. After a second unusable return, the official supplies a neutral rolling pass. An ordinary mistake does not change the score.

The defender has to kick the ball out to end the go. Winning possession in play does not by itself end the go. A shot or deflection behind the goal is also out. The official may stop and reset a stalled or unsafe duel.

02

Three each. Five each in the final.

A standard knockout match gives each player three attacks. They alternate through six attacks. The final gives each player five attacks.

Order
AlternateA then B, repeating

Player A attacks first, then Player B. They alternate until each has attacked three times in a standard match or five times in the final.

One designated goalkeeper is intended to stay in goal throughout the tournament. The same keeper always faces both players for the whole match.

Consistency for both players

If more than one suitable goalkeeper shares the event role, the same keeper remains in goal for all scheduled and sudden-death attacks in a match.

03

Goal-only scoring

Only a goal adds one point to a player’s total.

Goal
1 point for the attacker.
Defender kicks out
No point is awarded. Winning possession alone does not end the go.
Goalkeeper collection
No point is awarded. A rebound that stays in play continues.

There are no bonus points for skills, blocks, tackles or saves. A saved rebound continues while it remains in play; only a clean collection or the ball going out ends the go without a goal.

04

Straight-knockout result

This is straight knockout. The player with the most goals after the scheduled attacks advances to the next round. A level score moves to paired sudden death.

  • Every match is a straight-knockout match.
  • A standard match ends after three attacks each; the final ends after five attacks each.
  • The player with the most goals wins and advances to the next round.
  • If the scores are level, each player receives one extra attack in the same order.
  • If one scores and the other does not, the scorer advances.
  • If both have the same outcome, another paired round begins.
Equal chances in sudden death

Both players always complete an attack before the result is decided.

05

Child-friendly conduct

Keep the contest brave, safe and positive.

Ordinary mistakes

Stop, reset and play again without harsh automatic deductions.

Repeated deliberate fouls

Give a clear warning and let the pitch official manage the behaviour.

Dangerous conduct

The event lead may remove a child from play and return them to their parent or carer.

Touchline

Encouragement is welcome. Live technical instructions are not.
