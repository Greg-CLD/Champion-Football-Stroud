Working rules

The 1v1 in
sixty seconds.

Fast to explain. Fair to play. Still subject to rehearsal, host-club agreement and Gloucestershire football-authority guidance.

10sec
Start Qualifying Points Knockouts Conduct
01

Start the duel

The same start is used in qualifying and knockouts.

  1. Attacker passesPlay the ball to the defender.
  2. Defender returnsPlay a controlled, neutral pass back.
  3. Clock startsThe attacker’s first touch begins the ten seconds.
  4. Duel finishesGoal, defensive outcome, controlled save, foul stoppage or time.
If the return cannot be controlled

The official stops play before the clock starts and allows one retake. After a second unusable return, the official supplies a neutral rolling pass. An ordinary mistake does not lose a point.

02

Winner-stays qualifying

Four participants rotate through four clear roles.

1Holder

Defends the goal.

2Challenger

Attacks for ten seconds.

3Goalkeeper

Tries to control the save.

4Next

Gets ready to rotate in.

The decisive player becomes or remains holder. The losing duellist normally moves into goal while the goalkeeper and waiting participant advance through the rotation.

Two wins is the holder limit. After two consecutive wins, the rotation advances so everybody keeps receiving opportunities.

No specialist goalkeeper is recruited. Competing children rotate through the role.

03

Qualifying performance points

Attack, defence and goalkeeping can each earn one point.

Attacker
1 point for scoring a goal.
Defender
1 point for completing the approved defensive outcome.
Goalkeeper
1 point for controlling a save.
Still to test

What earns the defender’s point?

A closed rehearsal should choose the version children and officials understand most consistently.

A · Escape gate

Win controlled possession and carry the ball through a marked gate.

B · Boundary outcome

Legally force or shield the ball out of play.

The leading two participants enter the Cup pathway and the other two enter the Plate. A tied group is settled with equal mirrored attacks rather than subjective skill judging.

04

Cup and Plate knockouts

Equal attacking and defending chances decide every match.

  • Each match uses four paired rounds.
  • In every round, both children attack once and defend once under the same conditions.
  • Another competing child keeps goal for both attacks in the pair, then goalkeeper duty rotates.
  • Goals decide the match; qualifying points and spectacular skills do not create automatic advancement.
  • A tie moves to paired sudden death, preserving equal opportunities.
  • Every entrant receives a participation medal; Cup, Plate and Performance Mindset awards remain separate.
05

Child-friendly conduct

Keep the contest brave, safe and positive.

Ordinary mistakes

Stop, reset and play again without harsh automatic deductions.

Repeated deliberate fouls

Give a clear warning and let the pitch official manage the behaviour.

Dangerous conduct

The event lead may remove a child from play and return them to their parent or carer.

Touchline

Encouragement is welcome. Live technical instructions are not.

BraveryTry the action.
ResetBe ready again.
RespectCompete properly.
