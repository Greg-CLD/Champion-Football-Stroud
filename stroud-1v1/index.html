What parents need to know

The match format, safety commitments and registration status in one place.

U7/U8 head-to-head football

Stroud 1v1 is a one-on-one football tournament for U7 and U8 players. Two players take turns attacking and defending, with ten seconds for each attack.

Event preparation is under way

The first event is being prepared. Registration will open only after the host, safeguarding responsibilities and event arrangements are confirmed.

Six attacks in every match

Each player attacks three times and defends three times against the same opponent. One designated goalkeeper stays in goal throughout all six attacks.

Goals decide the winner

Every goal scores one point. Defending and saving stop the attacker scoring but earn no separate points. The player with the most goals advances; level scores use paired sudden-death attacks.

Safeguarding comes first

The event will open only after a host has confirmed a welfare lead, first-aid arrangements and clear reporting procedures. Coaches and other eligible adults working directly with children will need an in-date FA-accepted DBS check and appropriate current FA safeguarding training.

Date and venue details stay private
