FAQ

Questions parents might ask

The practical things families would want to know before the idea goes any further.

What is Stroud 1v1?

It is a proposed one-on-one football tournament for U7 and U8 players. Two players compete head-to-head, taking turns to attack and defend. The one-two start and ten-second limit are working rules to confirm through rehearsal.

Is this event happening now?

No. This is a concept preview for discussion and testing. No bookings are open.

What would my child actually do?

They would attack three times and defend three times against the same opponent. One designated goalkeeper stays in goal throughout the six attacks.

How is a match decided?

Every goal scores one point. Defending and saving stop the attacker scoring but do not earn separate points. The player with most goals advances; a tie goes to sudden death, with its exact sequence still to confirm.

How would children be safeguarded?

Before any event could run, it would need an approved host, a welfare lead, first-aid arrangements and clear reporting procedures. Coaches and other eligible adults working directly with children would need an in-date FA-accepted DBS check and appropriate current FA safeguarding training.

When and where would it happen?

No date or venue is set. If the idea progresses, approved operational details would be shared privately with parents and carers.

How do I register?

Registration is not open. The interest area below previews how parents and carers could register interest or ask a question later, but this website does not collect personal information.

Would children be filmed?

No official filming is proposed for the pilot concept.

What would it cost?

The working assumption is £15 per child, subject to feasibility and approval.

Interested in taking part?
