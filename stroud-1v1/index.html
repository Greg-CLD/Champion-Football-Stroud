Straight knockout. Win and move on.

This is a straight-knockout tournament: the player with the most goals after the scheduled attacks advances. Standard matches have three attacks each; the final has five. Level scores move to paired sudden death.

What parents need to know

The match format, safety commitments and registration status in one place.

U7/U8 head-to-head football

Stroud 1v1 is a one-on-one football tournament for U7 and U8 players. Standard matches give each player three attacks; the final gives each player five. Players take turns attacking and defending, with no time limit.

Event preparation is under way

The first event is being prepared. Registration will open only after the host, safeguarding responsibilities and event arrangements are confirmed.

One consistent goalkeeper throughout the event

The aim is for one designated goalkeeper to stay in goal throughout the tournament. If more than one suitable goalkeeper shares the role, the same keeper stays in goal for both players throughout each match.

Goals decide the winner

Every goal scores one point. A goalkeeper collection or a defender clearing the ball out ends the go without a point; a saved rebound stays live. This is straight knockout: the player with the most goals advances, and level scores use paired sudden-death attacks.

Safeguarding comes first

The event will open only after a host has confirmed a welfare lead, first-aid arrangements and clear reporting procedures. Coaches and other eligible adults working directly with children will need an in-date FA-accepted DBS check and appropriate current FA safeguarding training.

Date and venue details stay private

No date or venue is currently confirmed. Practical details will be shared privately with parents and carers once registration opens.

Registration is not open yet

This website does not collect personal information. Registration and enquiry routes will be added only after the event arrangements and safeguarding responsibilities are confirmed.

Media consent is separate and optional

With a parent or carer’s specific permission, selected official activity photos may be used on this website. Permission for any later social-media use will be asked separately and will remain optional. Saying no will not affect a child’s place or chance to take part. Children will not be named in public media, and matches will not be livestreamed.
